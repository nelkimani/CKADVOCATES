What to prepare before your first legal consultation

A first consultation is most useful when we can see the full picture quickly. A little preparation beforehand saves time and lets us focus on advice.

Bring the documents that relate to your matter

Gather anything in writing that touches the issue: agreements, letters, receipts, title documents, correspondence and any notices you have received. Copies are fine. If you are unsure whether something matters, bring it.

Write down the sequence of events

A simple timeline with dates, names and what was agreed or said helps us understand how the matter developed. Short notes are enough.

Decide what you want to achieve

Tell us the outcome you are hoping for, and any deadline you are working against. This lets us advise on the options that fit your goals.

List your questions

Bring a short list of questions, including practical ones about timing and cost. We would rather answer them at the start.

General information only. It is not legal advice for your particular situation.
